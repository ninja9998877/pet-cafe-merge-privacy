English

This policy explains how the iOS game Dock Cat Sort (bundle ID com.kachanali.sort, “the Game”) handles information. The Game is developed and provided by the individual developer 刘兆楠 (Liu Zhaonan), publishing as “咔嚓纳利工作室 / Kachanali Studio” (not a registered company) (“we”, “us”).

1. Summary

  • The Game has no accounts and never asks for your name, email, phone number or similar personal information.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • Your game progress is stored on your device.
  • This soft-launch version is free with advertising (Google AdMob: optional rewarded videos and interstitial ads). There are no in-app purchases in this version.
  • We use Google Firebase Analytics (gameplay statistics) and Firebase Crashlytics (crash / stability reports). Google AdMob serves and measures ads. They process data under their own policies, described below.
  • The Game is distributed worldwide excluding mainland China (and any other regions we remove in App Store Connect).

2. Data stored on your device

Level progress, settings and similar state are saved in the app’s local storage on your device. They are not uploaded to a game server of ours; we do not run an account database. Deleting the Game deletes this local data.

3. Analytics (Firebase Analytics)

  • We use Firebase Analytics to understand how the Game is played (for example which levels are started, cleared or failed, and whether helpers or ads were used) so we can improve the Game.
  • Example event names used in soft launch include: app_open, level_start, level_clear, level_fail, prop_use, rv_request / rv_show / rv_reward / rv_fail, interstitial_show / interstitial_fail (and similar funnel events). Events carry gameplay parameters such as level id — not your name or email.
  • The Firebase SDK also automatically collects basic device and app information (for example device model, OS version, app version, language/region, approximate country inferred from IP address) and a Firebase app-instance ID. According to Google, on iOS this may include the identifier for vendor (IDFV). If you allow tracking in Apple’s App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt, Firebase Analytics may also be able to use the advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • We do not send names, emails or payment details.
  • Soft launch: analytics collection is on by default. You can limit tracking via iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, contact us (section 10), or delete the Game.
  • For Firebase Analytics, Google acts as our data processor under Google’s Analytics / data-processing terms. For AdMob, Google acts as an independent controller.

4. Crash reporting (Firebase Crashlytics)

  • We use Firebase Crashlytics to learn about crashes and stability issues so we can fix them.
  • Crashlytics may collect crash logs, stack traces, device and app version information, and similar diagnostic data. Soft launch: Crashlytics collection is on by default.
  • We do not use Crashlytics to collect your name or email.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

  • The Game shows rewarded video ads (you choose to watch — for example for Undo / Hint / Extra Slot after free uses, or to continue after a fail) and interstitial ads that may appear later in the level list (early levels are designed to avoid them; Hard levels may skip interstitials per design).
  • There are no in-app purchases and no “remove ads” product in this version.
  • Before the first ad request that needs it, iOS may show the App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt. AdMob (and Analytics) can use the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking. Declining does not block core gameplay. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Where required (for example EEA / UK), Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) or similar consent UI may ask for ad-related consent; follow on-screen choices.
  • AdMob processes device information, IP address, ad interactions and related data to serve and measure ads. The AdMob SDK may also collect crash / performance diagnostics for ad measurement.
  • We may link AdMob with Firebase so estimated ad-impression value appears in analytics without your personal identity.

6. What this version does not do

  • No in-app purchases — no Apple IAP products, no purchase history collected by us for this Game.
  • No account / sign-in — no Game Center requirement, no email login.
  • No microphone / camera features in this soft-launch build.
  • No first-party game server, chat, UGC or social graph.
  • No remote content-update CDN claimed for this soft-launch build — levels ship in the app binary / local data.

7. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The Game has no chat, no social features and no accounts. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us and we will help delete it.

8. Data retention

  • Data on your device remains until you delete the Game.
  • Firebase Analytics retention follows Google’s product settings / defaults. Aggregated reports follow Google’s rules.
  • Crashlytics and AdMob retain data under Google’s policies.
  • We do not operate a separate account database of players.

9. Your choices

  • Tracking / IDFA: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Ads: rewarded videos are optional (you tap to watch). Interstitials may appear later in the level progression as described above.
  • Local data: delete the Game to remove progress stored on the device.
  • Requests: email us (section 10). Because we do not collect names or emails in-game, we may need information you can find on the device (for example approximate install time / app version) to locate analytics records. Deleting and reinstalling the Game also starts a new Firebase app-instance identifier.
