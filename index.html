中文

本政策说明 iOS 游戏 Pet Café Merge（Bundle ID com.ninja.petcafemerge，以下简称“本游戏”）如何处理信息。本游戏由个人开发者 刘兆楠（以“咔嚓纳利工作室 Kachanali Studio”名义发布，非注册公司，以下简称“我们”）开发和提供。

1. 概要

  • 本游戏没有账号系统，不要求你提供姓名、邮箱、电话等个人信息。
  • 我们不出售任何个人信息。
  • 游戏进度保存在你的设备上。
  • 我们使用 Google 的 Firebase Analytics（统计）和 Google AdMob（激励视频广告），二者按其各自政策处理数据，详见下文。

2. 设备上保存的数据

游戏进度、金币、已解锁内容、设置选项、已购内容记录等保存在你的设备本地（应用自身的存储空间），不会上传到我们的服务器。我们没有自己的游戏服务器或账号数据库。删除本游戏即会删除这些数据。

3. 统计（Firebase Analytics）

  • 我们用 Firebase Analytics 了解游戏玩法表现，以改进关卡和功能。发送的是游戏内事件，例如关卡开始/完成、领取奖励、广告观看结果、商店浏览与购买结果（商品编号、价格和币种）等。
  • Firebase SDK 还会自动收集设备和应用的基本信息（如设备型号、系统版本、应用版本、语言/地区、大致所在国家（由 IP 地址推断））以及 Firebase 的应用实例 ID；根据 Google 的说明，iOS 上还可能包括供应商标识符（IDFV）。本游戏使用的 Firebase Analytics 版本包含广告标识符支持：只有你在 ATT 弹窗中允许跟踪，Firebase Analytics 才可能同时收集广告标识符（IDFA）。
  • 我们不发送姓名、邮箱等个人信息，也不发送购买的交易编号或收据。
  • 你可以关闭统计：在游戏“设置”中关闭“使用统计”开关（默认开启）。关闭后游戏会停止 Firebase 统计收集（同时停止 AdMob 通过 Firebase 产生的广告展示统计），你可以随时重新打开。
  • 就 Firebase Analytics 而言，Google 作为我们的数据处理方，按照 Google Analytics 条款及 Google 数据处理条款，代表我们处理上述数据；就 AdMob 而言，Google 是独立的数据控制方。

4. 广告（Google AdMob）

  • 本游戏只有激励视频广告（你主动点击观看才播放，用于获得额外奖励），没有插屏广告。已购买“支持者礼包”的玩家不会看到广告，也不会被询问广告追踪。
  • 在首次请求广告前，iOS 会弹出“App 跟踪透明度”（ATT）询问。只有你选择允许，AdMob（以及第 3 条所述的 Firebase Analytics）才能读取广告标识符（IDFA）；拒绝也不影响游戏。你可以随时在 iOS“设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 跟踪”中更改。
  • 在欧洲经济区、英国等地区，游戏会通过 Google 的用户同意工具（UMP）征求你的同意；需要时，游戏“设置”里会出现“隐私选项”按钮，可随时修改选择。
  • AdMob 会为展示和衡量广告处理设备信息、IP 地址、广告互动等数据。我们开启了 AdMob 的“展示层级广告收入”功能，并将 AdMob 与 Firebase 关联，因此每次广告展示的收入估算会出现在我们的统计中（不含你的个人身份信息）。
  • AdMob SDK 还可能收集崩溃日志和性能数据（如启动时间、卡顿率），用于诊断问题和广告衡量。

5. 麦克风（“叫猫”功能）

  • “叫猫”功能在你允许时使用麦克风，只在你的设备上测量声音大小来判断你是否发出了叫声。
  • 我们不录音、不保存、不上传任何音频，也不使用语音识别。统计里只记录该功能被使用的次数和结果（例如“猫听到了”或“改用按钮”）。
  • 不授权麦克风也可以正常使用：直接点按钮即可叫猫。你也可以在游戏设置中关闭该功能，或在 iOS 设置中撤销麦克风权限。

6. 应用内购买

所有付款由 Apple 通过 App Store 处理。我们收不到你的付款信息（如银行卡号或 Apple ID 账户信息）。为防止重复发放和恢复购买，游戏会在设备本地保存 Apple 返回的交易记录，这些记录不会发送给我们。

7. 游戏内容更新下载（Cloudflare）

本游戏可能从我们托管在 Cloudflare R2 上的地址下载新的关卡、活动配置、外观等内容更新。下载时你的设备会像访问普通网站一样连接 Cloudflare 的服务器，Cloudflare 可能按其政策处理标准访问日志（如 IP 地址、访问时间、请求的文件）。下载请求中不包含你的设备标识符或个人信息。

8. 设备标识符（IDFV）

游戏设置中有一个隐藏的信息页面，用于测试人员查看本机的供应商标识符（IDFV）。该页面只在你的设备屏幕上显示，我们的游戏代码不会把它发送出去或写入统计。测试版（TestFlight）中，游戏会下载一份测试设备名单，在本机上比对是否属于测试设备，比对时不上传 IDFV。（另见第 3 条：Firebase SDK 本身可能按 Google 的政策收集 IDFV。）统计中只会记录本机是否属于测试渠道（是或否），不记录 IDFV。

9. 本地通知

如你允许，游戏会在离线收益已满时发送一条本地通知。通知在设备本地安排，不经过任何服务器。

10. 儿童

本游戏不以 13 岁以下儿童为目标用户，我们也不会故意收集 13 岁以下儿童的个人信息。如果你认为儿童向我们提供了个人信息，请联系我们，我们会配合删除。

11. 数据保留

  • 设备上的游戏数据会一直保留，直到你删除本游戏。
  • Google Analytics（Firebase）中，事件级数据保留 2 个月，用户级数据保留 14 个月，到期由 Google 自动删除；设备有新的活动时，用户级数据的 14 个月保留期会重新计算。汇总报表按 Google 的规则保留。
  • 我们已将 Google Analytics 关联到 Google BigQuery 数据导出（Google Cloud 上的数据仓库），以便分析游戏事件。导出的事件数据存放在我们的 Google Cloud 项目中，我们会按与上面相同的原则只保留分析所需的时间。
  • AdMob 和 Cloudflare 按各自的隐私政策保留其数据。

12. 你的权利

  • 你可以随时：在游戏中关闭“使用统计”；在 iOS 设置中拒绝或撤销跟踪、麦克风和通知权限；删除游戏以清除本地数据。
  • 如需查询、删除与你的设备相关的统计数据，或有任何隐私问题，请发邮件至 ninja9998877@gmail.com。由于我们不收集姓名或邮箱，可能需要你提供游戏内可查到的信息以便定位数据。我们会在合理时间内回复。

English

This policy explains how the iOS game Pet Café Merge (bundle ID com.ninja.petcafemerge, "the Game") handles information. The Game is developed and provided by the individual developer 刘兆楠 (Liu Zhaonan), publishing as "咔嚓纳利工作室 Kachanali Studio" (not a registered company) ("we", "us").

1. Summary

  • The Game has no accounts and never asks for your name, email, phone number or similar personal information.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • Your game progress is stored on your device.
  • We use Google Firebase Analytics (analytics) and Google AdMob (rewarded video ads); they process data under their own policies, described below.

2. Data stored on your device

Progress, coins, unlocked content, settings and records of purchased items are saved locally in the app's own storage on your device. They are not uploaded to our servers; we do not run a game server or account database. Deleting the Game deletes this data.

3. Analytics (Firebase Analytics)

  • We use Firebase Analytics to understand how the Game is played so we can improve levels and features. The Game sends in-game events such as level start/clear, reward claims, ad view outcomes, and shop views and purchase outcomes (product ID, price and currency).
  • The Firebase SDK also automatically collects basic device and app information (e.g. device model, OS version, app version, language/region, approximate country (inferred from the IP address)) and a Firebase app instance ID; according to Google, on iOS this may include the identifier for vendor (IDFV). The Firebase Analytics build used in the Game includes advertising-ID support: only if you allow tracking in the ATT prompt, Firebase Analytics may also collect the advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • We do not send names, emails or other personal information, and we do not send purchase transaction IDs or receipts.
  • You can turn analytics off: switch off "Usage analytics" in the Game's Settings (on by default). The Game then stops Firebase Analytics collection (which also stops the ad-impression statistics AdMob reports through Firebase). You can turn it back on at any time.
  • For Firebase Analytics, Google acts as our data processor under the Google Analytics Terms and data processing terms. For AdMob, Google acts as an independent controller.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

  • The Game shows rewarded video ads only — an ad plays only when you choose to watch one for a bonus. There are no interstitial ads. Players who own the Supporter Pack see no ads and are not asked about tracking.
  • Before the first ad request, iOS shows the App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt. AdMob (and Firebase Analytics, see section 3) can access the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow it; declining does not affect gameplay. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • In the EEA, the UK and similar regions, the Game asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP); where required, a "Privacy options" button appears in the Game's Settings so you can change your choice.
  • AdMob processes device information, IP address and ad interactions to serve and measure ads. We have enabled AdMob's impression-level ad revenue feature and linked AdMob to Firebase, so an estimated revenue value for each ad impression appears in our analytics (without your personal identity).
  • The AdMob SDK may also collect crash logs and performance data (such as app launch time and hang rate) to diagnose problems and measure ads.

5. Microphone ("call the cat")

  • If you allow it, the "call the cat" feature uses the microphone only to measure loudness on your device to detect your call.
  • We never record, store or upload audio and do not use speech recognition. Analytics only count how often the feature is used and its outcome (e.g. "the cat heard you" or "used the button").
  • Microphone access is optional: you can always call the cat with a button. You can also turn the feature off in the Game's Settings or revoke microphone access in iOS Settings.

6. In-app purchases

All payments are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment information (such as card numbers or Apple ID account details). To prevent duplicate grants and to restore purchases, the Game keeps Apple's transaction records locally on your device; they are not sent to us.

7. Content update downloads (Cloudflare)

The Game may download content updates (new levels, event settings, cosmetic items and similar) from storage we host on Cloudflare R2. As with visiting any website, your device connects to Cloudflare's servers, and Cloudflare may process standard access logs (such as IP address, time and the file requested) under its own policy. These download requests contain no device identifier or personal information.

8. Device identifier (IDFV)

A hidden information page in Settings lets testers view this device's identifier for vendor (IDFV). It is shown only on your screen; the Game's code does not transmit it or log it to analytics. In TestFlight builds, the Game downloads a list of test devices and compares it on the device; the IDFV is not uploaded for this check. (See also section 3: the Firebase SDK itself may collect the IDFV under Google's policies.) Analytics only record whether this device is in the test channel (yes or no); the IDFV is not recorded.

9. Local notifications

If you allow notifications, the Game may send one local notification when your offline earnings are full. It is scheduled on your device and does not go through any server.

10. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us and we will help delete it.

11. Data retention

  • Game data on your device is kept until you delete the Game.
  • In Google Analytics (Firebase), event-level data is retained for 2 months and user-level data for 14 months, after which Google deletes it automatically; the 14-month user-data period restarts when the device shows new activity. Aggregated reports are kept under Google's rules.
  • We have linked Google Analytics to Google BigQuery export (a data warehouse on Google Cloud) to analyse game events. Exported event data is stored in our Google Cloud project, and we keep it only as long as needed for analysis, on the same principles as above.
  • AdMob and Cloudflare keep their data according to their own privacy policies.

12. Your choices and rights

  • At any time you can turn off "Usage analytics" in the Game; deny or revoke tracking, microphone and notification permissions in iOS Settings; and delete the Game to remove local data.
  • To ask about or request deletion of analytics data related to your device, or for any privacy question, email ninja9998877@gmail.com. Because we do not collect names or emails, we may need information you can find in the Game to locate the data. We will reply within a reasonable time.
